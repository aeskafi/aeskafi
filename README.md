With a background as a CTO and technical leader, I’ve shifted away from slow enterprise roadmaps to lean, agile **AI-driven Vibe Coding**—shipping full-stack products from scratch in days, not months.

- ⚡ **Currently:** Helping founders ship production-ready Web/AI MVPs in 5–10 days via [arham.dev](https://arham.dev).

- 🕊️ **Mission:** Actively using our voice and tech to advocate for digital sovereignty, uncensored internet access, and human rights for the courageous people of Iran and communities under authoritarian oppression.
